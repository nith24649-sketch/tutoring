Common questions

Before you ask

Is the placement test really free?

Yes — free and no obligation. It takes about twenty minutes online, and every result is reviewed personally before any group recommendation. It's how groups get built by level rather than just by year.

How do groups stay small?

Every group is capped at five students — no exceptions. When a group is full, the timetable shows it as waitlist-only, and you'll be offered the next place in the right group in order.

What happens if we're away one week?

Mark the date on the portal calendar (one click) and Nith sees it straight away, so a make-up session can be planned instead of the week being lost.

Do siblings get a discount?

Yes — 10% off the second child's term fees.

How does payment work?

Itemised invoices appear in the portal under Parent view. Pay by bank transfer or PayID with the invoice number as the reference, and a receipt is sent once payment clears. Fees are due at the end of week 1 each term.

What's the AI Study Helper — and is it doing the homework?

No. It explains the steps and the method between sessions, then leaves the final step to the student. Anything important still gets checked with Nith at the next session.
